Pinoy TV Live

--:--Manila time

Works with a TV remote or keyboard: ←→↑↓ to move, OK/Enter to watch, Back/Esc to return. Number keys jump straight to a channel. When the app opens it checks every channel on your device and hides the ones that cannot play here; use Show hidden to see them anyway.

TV and TeleRadyo channels are the broadcasters' own free, official livestreams. AM/FM stations come from the open Radio Browser directory (the same public station list many radio apps use) and play as audio inside this app. When a network isn't broadcasting live, you'll see its latest stream or a "not live" notice. Networks decide where their streams can play, so some may be unavailable outside the Philippines or outside YouTube; the app marks those for you.
