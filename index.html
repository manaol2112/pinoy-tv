Pinoy TV Live

--:--Manila time

Works with a TV remote or keyboard: ←→↑↓ to move, OK/Enter to watch, Back/Esc to return. Number keys jump straight to a channel.

Every stream here is the network's own free, official livestream, played inside this app. When a network isn't broadcasting live, you'll see its latest stream or a "not live" notice. Networks decide where their streams can play, so some may be unavailable outside the Philippines or outside YouTube; the app marks those for you.
